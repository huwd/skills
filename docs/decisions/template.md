# NNNN. Short title in sentence case, naming the choice

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-slug.md)
- **Decided by:** @handle, @handle
- **Decided:** YYYY-MM-DD, or YYYY-MM if only the month is known
- **Recorded:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Discussion:** #issue, #PR

## Summary

In the context of {situation}, facing {problem or need}, the decision was for
{chosen option} and against {main alternatives}, to achieve {benefit},
accepting {cost or downside}.

## Context

What was true at the time: the problem, the constraints, and what forced a
choice. Write it so someone who wasn't there can follow it.

## Options considered

### {Chosen option} (chosen)

What it is, with its main advantages and disadvantages.

### {Alternative}

What it is, and why it lost.

## Decision

The decision, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision: what gets easier, what gets harder, what
is accepted as a cost, and anything to revisit later or watch for.
